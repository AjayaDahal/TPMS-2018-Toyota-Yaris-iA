# Design audit: TPMS monitor panel

**Date:** 2026-11-04

**Scope:**
- The schematic (`tpms_panel.kicad_sch`).
- The board (`tpms_panel.kicad_pcb`).
- The exported Gerber/drill set.

All three are generated by `scripts/` (encoder variant).

> **⚠ Reminder: not ready to order yet.** The panel dimensions are still scaled from the report image. Send the real
> dimensions first. Until `DIMENSIONS_CONFIRMED = True` in `panel_config.py`, the Gerber zip is named
> `tpms_panel_gerbers_jlcpcb_DRAFT_dims_unconfirmed.zip`.

## Result

- **No wrong connections were found.** Every interface matches its datasheet, and all 46 nets / 54 parts were checked pin by pin.
- **Nine issues were found and fixed** (table below). After the fixes, the regenerated project gives:
  - ERC: 0 errors / 0 warnings.
  - DRC, run with JLCPCB's limits: 0 errors / 0 warnings / 0 unconnected / 0 parity issues.
  - An independent re-read of the Gerber and drill files passed.
- **What's left:** a few mechanical checks for you and some documented trade-offs.

## How it was checked

| Area | Method |
|---|---|
| Connectivity | Exported the netlist and reviewed every net and pin against the intended circuit |
| Pinouts | Compared against datasheets: Newhaven NHD-2.7-12864WDW3 (rev 6, 2024), EBYTE E07-M1101D-SMA pin table, DOIT ESP32 DevKit V1, TRACO TSR 1, AMS1117, Littelfuse SMAJ, Bourns MF-MSMF, Espressif ESP32 datasheet v5.3. Polarity of KiCad symbols/footprints checked for the buzzer, LEDs, diodes, tantalum, MMBT3904 and EC11 |
| Ratings | Voltage, current, power and logic-level calculations (see below) |
| Mechanics | Script check of every through-hole pin against parts on the other side of the board, plus clearances (knob, display flex, USB plug, SMA, mounting holes) |
| Manufacturing | DRC with JLCPCB's published 2-layer limits, plus an annular-ring scan of every plated pad |
| Gerbers | Re-parsed the exported Gerber/Excellon files with an independent parser (gerbonara plus a small Excellon reader), not KiCad |

## Fixed

| # | Finding | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C1 (U3 input capacitor) was rated 35 V | TRACO requires 22 µF / 50 V at the TSR 1 input above 32 V, and the TVS can clamp up to 32–35.5 V | C1 → 47 µF / 50 V, Panasonic EEE-FK1H470XP (same 6.3 mm footprint) |
| 2 | D6 SMAJ20CA breaks down at 22.2–24.5 V | A 24 V jump start would drive ~1 A (~23 W) through an SMA TVS rated 3.3 W, and it would fail short | D6 → SMAJ22CA: 24.4 V minimum breakdown, 35.5 V maximum clamp (U3 is rated to 36 V) |
| 3 | The RECOM R-78E5.0-1.0 was listed as an alternative for U3 | It is only rated for 8–28 V input | Removed. Alternatives must be rated ≥ 36 V, e.g. Mornsun K7805-1000R3 (8–36 V) |
| 4 | C5 (AMS1117 output) was a 22 µF ceramic | The AMS1117 datasheet specifies 22 µF solid tantalum for stability. Low-ESR ceramics alone can make some AMS1117s oscillate | C5 → 22 µF / 10 V tantalum, case A (KEMET T491A226K010AT) |
| 5 | U4's tab had no heatsinking copper | With 100 % of pixels lit, the OLED draws 345 mA (375 mA max), which is up to 0.67 W in U4 | Added a 124 mm² +3V3 copper pour on the tab (rough estimate: from about 110 to about 75 °C/W) |
| 6 | 58 silkscreen texts were 0.8–0.9 mm high, two of them with 0.12 mm strokes | JLCPCB says text below 1.0 mm high or with strokes under 0.15 mm is unreadable | All text is now ≥ 1.0 mm / 0.15 mm, and DRC treats smaller text as an error |
| 7 | One via was 0.6/0.4 mm (0.1 mm annular ring) | Specctra session files carry no drill sizes, so KiCad used the Power net-class drill | The build now re-drills such vias to 0.3 mm, and the DRC minimum via ring is 0.15 mm |
| 8 | MX-05V divider was 2.2 k / 10 k | The LM358 output only reaches ~3.0–3.7 V, giving 2.5–3.0 V at IO36. The ESP32 needs 0.75 × 3.3 = 2.48 V for a logic high, so this was marginal | R9 → 1 k, giving 2.7–3.4 V (below the 3.6 V absolute maximum) |
| 9 | The display hardware was given as M2.5 | The NHD mounting holes are Ø2.5 mm, so M2.5 screws don't pass | Docs: M2 screws with M2 × 3 mm spacers |

Smaller changes in the same pass:
- DRC hole-to-hole minimum raised to JLCPCB's 0.45 mm.
- Schematic note added: use a WROOM-32 DevKit (on WROVER boards, IO16/IO17 are taken by PSRAM).
- Firmware notes added to the README.

## Verified correct (no change needed)

- **OLED** (NHD datasheet rev 6):
  - The pin table matches the footprint.
  - Interface pins are set for 4-wire SPI: BS1 = BS0 = 0.
  - Unused serial-mode pins are tied as the datasheet says: pins 5–6 and 10–14 to VSS, pin 9 left open.
  - /SHDN has an internal pull-up, so leaving it open is fine.
  - VDD is 3.0–3.5 V.
  - Pin 1 is on the right as seen from the front.
  - The header is centred, 2.50 mm below the top edge, and the holes are on a 77 × 42.5 mm pitch.
  - JP1 is safe either way: pin 3 is not connected inside the module in its default jumper setting.
- **CC1101 (E07)**: the pin table matches U1. MISO goes high-impedance when CSn is high (IOCFG1 reset value 0x2E), so sharing the SPI bus with the OLED works.
- **ESP32 DevKit**:
  - Pin order and orientation are correct.
  - The DevKit's 3V3 pin is not paralleled with U4.
  - Strapping pins: IO2 is on J4 (documented), IO5 and IO15 drive chip selects (fine), and IO12 is unused.
  - The input-only pins IO34/35/36/39 are used only as inputs.
  - No pin ever sees more than the ESP32's absolute maximum of VDD + 0.3 V.
- **Encoder**:
  - A/B: 10 k pull-up plus a 10 k / 10 nF filter (0.1–0.2 ms).
  - Push switch: 10 k / 100 nF debounce (1 ms).
  - Both are fast enough for quick spins.
- **Buzzer driver**:
  - MMBT3904 pinout is correct (1 = base, 2 = emitter, 3 = collector).
  - 2.5 mA of base drive saturates it at the buzzer's ~30 mA.
  - The flyback diode polarity is correct, and the buzzer's "+" is on pad 1.
- **LEDs**: the cathode is the square pad, and they get ~6 mA with red or amber LEDs.
- **Power path**:
  - The chain is PTC → TVS → SS34 (blocks reverse battery) → TSR → AMS1117.
  - 5 V budget: worst case is about 0.8 A against the TSR's 1 A (ESP32 Wi-Fi peaks ~0.4 A, OLED 0.375 A, radio and buzzer). Typical is about 0.25 A.
  - At 12 V, that is at most 0.37 A in, which is under the PTC hold current even at 85 °C (0.43 A).
- **Board**:
  - Power tracks are 0.6 mm (0.45 mm at one SOT-223 pin).
  - There is no copper under the ESP32 antenna.
  - Copper keep-outs are in place around the USB cut-outs, and the mounting holes are clear of copper.
  - The knob is 4.25 mm from the display.
  - The display's flex cable is 1.8 mm from the LEDs.
- **JLCPCB manufacturing limits**:
  - Plated-hole annular rings are ≥ 0.20 mm. The EC11 mounting slots come from the stock KiCad footprint.
  - Vias are 0.6/0.3 and 0.8/0.4 mm.
  - The minimum track is 0.25 mm and the edge clearance 0.4 mm.
- **Gerbers**:
  - 14 files, each identified by its X2 file function (copper L1/L2, masks, legends, paste, profile).
  - The outline is closed and measures 191 × 129 mm.
  - All 270 plated holes and 2 plated slots have pads on both layers.
  - None of the 8 non-plated holes has copper on it.

## For you to check (mechanical)

1. **Display stack-up.**
   - The ESP32 socket pins come through under the display, and the display's back has parts up to ~2.1 mm tall.
   - Trim those pins to ≤ 1 mm and use M2 × 3 mm spacers. That leaves ≈ 0.9 mm of clearance.
2. **ESP32 USB cable.** The micro-USB plug has ~34 mm of straight room before it reaches the CC1101 module. Use a short-bodied or 90° cable, or update over the air.
3. **Antenna.** The SMA ends ~2 mm inside the top edge, so the antenna sticks out past the panel. Plan the dash space, or use a right-angle antenna or an SMA extension cable.
4. **Depth behind the panel.** Allow about 17 mm behind the PCB for the DevKit on its sockets and the CC1101 with its SMA, plus the antenna.
5. **Front appearance.** The through-hole joints of back-side parts show on the front face: the CC1101 socket, J1, U3, the buzzer, and the holes for the unpopulated J2/J3/J4.

## Accepted trade-offs (hobby-grade automotive)

- **Load dump and large transients.** A 400 W SMA TVS can't absorb an unsuppressed load dump, and U3 is only rated to 36 V.
  - Most modern cars already clamp load dumps centrally to about 35 V.
  - Full ISO 16750 robustness would need a ≥ 60 V regulator and a TVS around 33 V.
  - The likely failure is a shorted TVS: the PTC then trips and the board turns off. It is not a fire risk, provided the ACC tap is fused.
- **PTC voltage rating.** The MF-MSMF075/24X is rated 24 V, so it can be over-stressed if it trips during a > 24 V event.
- **EMI.** The TSR 1 meets EN 55032 class A only with an external filter (TRACO's EMC filter proposal). If you hear interference on AM radio, add that filter in the 12 V lead.
- **AMS1117 heat.** Even with the copper pour, U4 runs warm in the worst case (full-white screen in a hot car). A mostly dark UI is fine; otherwise use NHD jumper option #1 and close JP1.
